Bright Spots map

Find schools making unusual progress.

The 2025 map identifies North Carolina public schools whose chronic absenteeism rates were substantially lower than predicted after accounting for school and socioeconomic context. Use it to explore where Bright Spots are located and how they are distributed across grade bands.

2025 Attendance Bright Spots. Select school markers and use the map controls to refine the view.Open map in a new window

How to use the map

Start broad, then narrow your view.

The map is designed for quick exploration. It shows both the strength of each Bright Spot signal and the grade band of the school.

1

Filter the signal

Use the checkboxes to show or hide Exceptional, Strong, and Promising Bright Spots.

2

Read the colors

Marker colors indicate grade band, helping you scan for elementary, middle, high, and combined schools.

3

Select a school

Click a marker to inspect a school and use the map to compare its location with nearby Bright Spots.

4

Go deeper

Use the dashboard for additional school-level exploration or read a Spotlight when one is available.

Interpret the map as a discovery tool. A Bright Spot designation means a school performed substantially better than the model predicted for its context. It does not establish why the school improved or prove that a particular practice caused the result.
